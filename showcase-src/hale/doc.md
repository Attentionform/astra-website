# Master Services Agreement

## 1. Services

The Supplier shall provide the services described in each Statement of Work (the Services) to the Customer on the terms of this Agreement.

## 2. Fees and payment

The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty days of receipt. Late payments accrue interest at the rate agreed in the Statement of Work.

## 3. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep the other party Confidential Information secret and use it only to perform this Agreement. These obligations continue for five years after termination.

## 4. Limitation of liability

Neither party total liability under this Agreement shall exceed the fees paid in the twelve months before the claim arose.

## 5. Term and termination

This Agreement continues for an initial term of two years. Either party may terminate it on sixty days written notice.

## 6. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
