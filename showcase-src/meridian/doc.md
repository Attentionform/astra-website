# Meridian Orbital: Constellation Launch Brief

## 1. Mission overview

Meridian will place **24 observation satellites** in three orbital planes over 18 months, giving daily revisit over every major shipping lane. This brief sets out the schedule, the cost envelope and the single decision needed from the programme board.[^1]

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Launch window A closes on 3 March. Contracts for the second launch vehicle must be signed by 20 January to hold the slot.

## 3. Where the budget goes

![Budget by category](charts/budget.json)

- **Launch services** are the largest line and the most schedule-sensitive.
- **Ground segment** is fully funded through the end of Plane B.
- **Contingency** is held centrally and released by the board.

> [!TIP]
> Sharing a launch between Planes B and C would release budget for a fourth ground station without moving the delivery date.

## 5. Risks

> [!WARNING]
> Both candidate launch vehicles share one upper-stage supplier. A single qualification delay would move Planes B and C together.

1. Upper-stage supply concentration.
2. Spectrum licence for the downlink band, decision due in February.
3. Thermal margin on the Plane A bus in the final test campaign.
