# Atlas Robotics: Q3 2026 Board Report

## 1. Executive summary

Revenue reached **$14.2M** in Q3, up **34.2%** year over year, led by platform subscriptions and the first full quarter of the Munich fulfilment deployment.[^1] Operating margin rose to **21.4%**, the fifth consecutive quarter of expansion.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The board is asked to approve the $2.5M warehouse-vision expansion in section 5 before the 14 November close.

## 3. Where the revenue comes from

![Q3 revenue mix](charts/mix.json)

- **Platform subscriptions** grew 41% and now carry 76% of revenue.
- **Services** grew 17%, mostly onboarding for the Munich site.
- **Licences** are flat and are being phased out of new contracts.

> [!TIP]
> Customers who adopt the platform inside 30 days of installation renew at 131%. Onboarding speed is the lever to protect.

## 4. Risks

> [!WARNING]
> Two sensor suppliers account for 64% of bill-of-materials cost. A single delay would move the Q4 delivery plan.

1. Supplier concentration in vision sensors.
2. Dependence on the Munich reference site for four open enterprise deals.
3. Currency exposure on euro-denominated contracts, now 38% of revenue.
